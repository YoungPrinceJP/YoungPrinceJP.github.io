THE STORY

Primordia follows Aurel, a young boy who discovers an ancient power within himself — a power that could change the fate of his world.

As the Shadow Legion rises and forgotten forces awaken, Aurel must uncover the truth about his past, his connection to the ancient tree, and the perilous destiny that awaits him.
