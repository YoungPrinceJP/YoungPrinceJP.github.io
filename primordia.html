The Beginning

A world was never meant to be perfect.

There is a hidden world shaped by forces older than kingdoms and civilizations. Nature, power and ancient laws exist in a balance that has survived for ages.

But balance does not mean peace.

In a hidden mountain settlement lives a twelve-year-old boy named Aurel.

Calm, curious and strangely different from the people around him, Aurel knows almost nothing about his true origin.

The elderly warrior who raised him has kept the truth hidden for years.

Then one day, something awakens.

"There is nothing perfect in this world. They will always be chaos."

A dying tree becomes the first sign that Aurel carries something the world has forgotten.

And far beyond the settlement, an ancient enemy notices.

PRIMORDIA — Chapter 1

The Awakening

Before the world knew his name, nature knew him.

Scene 1 — The Mountain Settlement
A hidden mountain settlement — Morning

High among the mountains, far from the great cities, a small settlement wakes beneath the morning mist.

Wooden homes stand between enormous trees. Smoke rises slowly from cooking fires. Children move through the narrow paths while adults begin another ordinary day.

Among them is a twelve-year-old boy.

Aurel.

He watches the forest more than he watches the people.

Aurel: Father, why do the trees here never lose all their leaves?
Guardian: Because they have learned how to survive the mountain.
Aurel: Can trees learn?
Guardian: More than you think.

Aurel looks at him.

Aurel: You always answer my questions with another mystery.
Guardian: And you always ask questions you are not ready to hear the answers to.

Aurel smiles.

Scene 2 — The Dying Tree
Forest edge — Afternoon

Later that day, Aurel wanders deeper into the forest.

He finds an old tree lying almost lifeless against the earth.

Its bark is cracked. Its branches are weak. Its leaves have fallen.

Aurel kneels beside it.

Aurel: Are you dying?

He places one hand against the trunk.

Nothing happens.

Aurel closes his eyes.

Aurel: Please...

The wind stops.

The forest becomes strangely silent.

Then—

A faint pulse moves through Aurel's hand.

Aurel opens his eyes.

New leaves begin appearing along the dead branches.

The cracked bark slowly closes.

Green spreads across the tree.

Aurel pulls his hand away in shock.

Aurel: What... did I just do?

Somewhere far away, something ancient stirs.

Scene 3 — The Shrine
Deep within the mountain — Evening

That evening, Aurel follows a strange feeling into an ancient part of the mountain.

Hidden behind stone and roots is an old shrine.

At its center stands a massive tree.

Around it are four mysterious symbols.

Aurel slowly approaches.

Aurel: I've never seen this place before.

Beneath the tree, something catches his attention.

A small stone lies partially buried beneath the roots.

Four symbols are carved into its surface.

Aurel reaches toward it.

The moment his fingers touch the stone—

A deep pulse shakes the shrine.

Aurel immediately pulls his hand away.

Aurel: No...

He looks around.

Something about the object frightens him.

Aurel pushes the stone back beneath the roots.

He hides it exactly where he found it.

He does not know what the artifact is.

He only knows that it should not be discovered.

Scene 4 — The Awakening Is Seen
Somewhere beyond the mountains — Night

Darkness.

A vast chamber lit only by faint shadows.

A figure stands before an ancient stone structure.

The atmosphere changes.

Dark Lord: So...

Silence.

Dark Lord: After all these years... the awakening has finally begun.

A shadow moves behind him.

Dark Lord: Find the boy.

No answer comes.

The Dark Lord turns toward the darkness.

Dark Lord: Bring him to me.

His eyes narrow.

Dark Lord: Before the ancient one wakes.

Aurel thinks he has only discovered a dying tree.

He is wrong.

Something inside him has awakened.

And somewhere beyond the mountains, the Shadow Legion has begun to move.
