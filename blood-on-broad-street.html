BLOOD ON
BROAD STREET

Before the blood, there was friendship. Three boys growing up in Lagos City never imagined that one ordinary day would become the beginning of a story that would change their lives forever.

The Story

BLOOD ON BROAD STREET is a Lagos City crime drama about friendship, survival, loyalty, betrayal and the consequences of choices made too early.

At the beginning, Tariq, Slim and Emeka are only ten-year-old boys trying to enjoy childhood in the middle of a busy Lagos neighborhood.

They have no money, no power and no idea what the future holds.

But they have something they value more than anything: each other.

"Before the blood... there was friendship."

Their story begins with something so simple that none of them could possibly understand what it would eventually become.

Episode 1

The Three Boys

Scene 1 — Before the Blood

Lagos City — Late Afternoon

The streets are alive. Hawkers call out to passing vehicles. Danfo buses move through traffic. Children play nearby while adults go about their business.

Three ten-year-old boys stand together on a quiet stretch near the roadside.

Tariq, Slim and Emeka have been friends for as long as they can remember.

They have no money. They have no power. They have no idea what the future will bring.

But they have each other.

Tariq: Look at you two. Both of you are scared already.
Slim: Scared of what? You? Please.
Emeka: Both of you talk too much. Just throw the stone.

Tariq picks up a small stone from the ground and studies it like a professional athlete.

Tariq: Bet you can't throw yours farther than mine.
Slim: That is the easiest bet you have ever made.
Emeka: Just don't cry when you lose.

The boys laugh.

One after another, they throw their stones.

The first stone flies.

The second goes farther.

Then Tariq takes his turn.

He throws.

The stone travels through the air...

SPLASH!

The boys stare toward the water.

Slim: Ha! I told you!
Emeka: You only got lucky.
Tariq: Lucky? Say it again.

They burst into laughter.

For a few minutes, they are just children.

Nothing else matters.

But none of them knows that the friendship they are celebrating today will eventually be tested by money, violence, secrets and blood.
