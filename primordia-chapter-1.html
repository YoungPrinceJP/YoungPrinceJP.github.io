Scene 1

The Mountain That Remembers

[Early morning. Mist covers a vast mountain range. Birds fly between enormous trees. Far above the clouds, ancient mountains surround a small hidden settlement.]
There are places the world has forgotten.
[The settlement slowly comes alive. Smoke rises from small houses. Villagers carry baskets. Children run through the narrow paths.]
And there are secrets that have remained hidden for generations.
[A boy suddenly races through the forest, jumping over rocks and roots.]
AUREL
"Wait!"
[He runs faster.]
AUREL
"Come back!"
[A small bird flies from one branch to another. Aurel stops beneath the tree, breathing heavily.]
AUREL
"...You!"
[The bird tilts its head.]
AUREL
"You made me run all the way here for nothing."
[The bird suddenly flies away.]
AUREL
"Hey!"
"Fine. Go."
[Aurel laughs. He looks deeper into the forest. His smile slowly fades. The trees ahead are larger, older, and covered in thick vines.]
AUREL
"I've never been this far."
"Maybe there's something interesting over there."
VOICE
"Aurel."
[Aurel freezes.]
AUREL
"...Oh no."
[He slowly turns around. An elderly man stands behind him, watching silently. His face is calm, but his eyes are sharp.]
GUARDIAN
"How many times have I told you not to come this far?"
AUREL
"Three."
[The guardian raises an eyebrow.]
AUREL
"...Maybe four."
GUARDIAN
"Four."
AUREL
"Okay. Four."
[The guardian looks toward the dark forest. His expression suddenly becomes serious.]
GUARDIAN
"You shouldn't be here."
AUREL
"Why?"
GUARDIAN
"Because there are things in these mountains you don't understand."
AUREL
"You always say that."
GUARDIAN
"Because you never stop asking."
[Aurel smiles.]
AUREL
"Maybe one day you'll tell me the truth."
[The guardian becomes silent. For a brief moment, his eyes reveal something painful.]
GUARDIAN
"Perhaps."
AUREL
"What does that mean?"
GUARDIAN
"It means we're going home."
AUREL
"But I just got here."
GUARDIAN
"Aurel."
AUREL
"Fine."
[They begin walking back toward the settlement.]
[Aurel walks ahead. The guardian remains behind for a moment and looks toward the forest. His expression changes.]
[A memory flashes through his mind. An ancient shrine buried deep within the mountains. Broken stone pillars surround a gigantic tree. At the center is a mysterious symbol—a tree surrounded by four symbols. A newborn child lies beneath the tree.]
YOUNG GUARDIAN
"...A child?"
[The memory disappears. The guardian closes his eyes.]
GUARDIAN — INNER THOUGHT
Not yet.
AUREL
"Are you coming?"
GUARDIAN
"Yes."
[He catches up with Aurel.]
[They disappear into the morning mist.]
[Deep beneath the ancient mountain, inside a forgotten shrine...]
SFX: CRK...
[An ancient stone develops a tiny crack. Something moves behind it. The four symbols carved into the stone glow faintly for a moment... then disappear.]
For twelve years, the mountain had kept its secret.
But that secret was beginning to awaken.
