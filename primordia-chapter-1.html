PRIMORDIA • CHAPTER 01

THE AWAKENING

Before the world knew his name, nature knew him.

The first chapter of Aurel's journey begins in a hidden mountain settlement surrounded by an ancient forest.

CHAPTER 01

The Awakening

"There is something about this forest... something I have always felt."

SCENE 01

The Mountain Settlement

A hidden mountain settlement — Morning

High among the mountains, far from the great cities, a small settlement wakes beneath the morning mist.

Wooden homes stand between enormous trees. Smoke rises slowly from cooking fires. Children move through the narrow paths while adults begin another ordinary day.

The mountains seem peaceful. The forest seems silent. But something ancient is watching.

Among the people of the settlement lived a twelve-year-old boy named Aurel.

Aurel was different from the other children. Not because he was stronger. Not because he understood the world better.

He simply felt something that nobody else seemed to notice.

The trees. The wind. The rivers hidden beneath the mountain.

Sometimes it felt as if the entire forest was breathing with him.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 02

The Dying Tree

Deep within the forest

Later that morning, Aurel wandered beyond the settlement and into the forest.

He had always been drawn there.

The deeper he walked, the quieter the world became.

Then he saw it.

The Tree

An enormous ancient tree stood alone beneath the mist. Its branches were dry. Its leaves had almost completely fallen.

Aurel stepped closer.

He placed his hand against the rough bark.

Aurel: "You're dying..."

He lowered his eyes.

Aurel: "I'm sorry."

For a moment, nothing happened.

Then the wind stopped.

A strange warmth moved through Aurel's hand.

Beneath his palm, something answered.

The Awakening

A faint green light travelled through the ancient tree. One branch moved. Then another.

New leaves slowly appeared.

The dry branches became green.

Life returned to the tree.

Aurel stared in disbelief.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 03

The Ancient Shrine

Beneath the mountain forest

The strange event did not go unnoticed.

Something in the mountains had awakened.

That evening, Aurel followed a faint path deeper into the forest and discovered an ancient shrine hidden between enormous trees.

At the centre of the shrine stood a symbol unlike anything he had ever seen.

The Symbol

A great tree surrounded by four mysterious symbols.

Aurel moved closer.

Beneath the ancient tree symbol, he noticed something partly buried beneath the stone.

He carefully pulled it free.

It was a small stone artifact marked with four symbols.

The moment he touched it, a strange cold passed through his body.

Aurel looked around.

He did not understand what the artifact was. But something inside him told him that it should remain hidden.

He placed it back beneath the stone.

Then he heard footsteps.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 04

The Shadow Arrives

The mountain settlement — Night

Night fell over the settlement.

Then the silence was broken.

A strange darkness moved through the trees.

The Shadow Legion had arrived.

Guardian: "Aurel!"

Aurel: "Father?"

Guardian: "You need to leave. Now."

Aurel had never heard that fear in his guardian's voice before.

The old man knew exactly who had come.

And he knew why they were there.

Run.

The settlement that had been Aurel's home was about to become a battlefield.

The guardian pushed Aurel toward the forest.

Aurel hesitated.

He looked back.

The first buildings were already burning.

Through the smoke, he saw his guardian standing alone against the approaching soldiers.

Aurel wanted to go back.

But his guardian looked toward him one last time.

Guardian: "LIVE, AUREL!"

Aurel ran.

He ran through the trees with tears in his eyes.

Behind him, the settlement disappeared beneath smoke and fire.

And somewhere in the darkness, something ancient had begun to awaken.

TO BE CONTINUED

Aurel has lost his home. His guardian is gone. The mysterious artifact remains hidden. And somewhere beyond the mountains, the Shadow Legion has begun its search.
