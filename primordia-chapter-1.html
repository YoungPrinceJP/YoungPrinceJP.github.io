PRIMORDIA • CHAPTER 01

THE AWAKENING

Before the world knew his name, nature knew him.

The first chapter of Aurel's journey. A hidden settlement. An ancient shrine. A power that should have remained forgotten.

CHAPTER 01

The Awakening

"There is something inside me... something I don't understand."

SCENE 01

The Mountain Settlement

Hidden mountain settlement — Morning

High among the mountains, far from the great cities, a small settlement wakes beneath the morning mist.

Wooden homes stand between enormous trees. Smoke rises slowly from cooking fires. Children run through narrow paths while the adults begin another ordinary day.

The mountains are peaceful. The forest is quiet.

But beneath that peace, something ancient is beginning to stir.

Among the children of the settlement is a twelve-year-old boy.

Aurel

A quiet boy with curious eyes who has always felt strangely connected to the forest.

Aurel walks beyond the houses, carrying a small bundle of firewood.

He stops.

The wind moves through the trees.

Aurel looks around.

Aurel: "Did you hear that?"

There is no answer.

Aurel: "I know you're there."

The leaves tremble gently although there is almost no wind.

Aurel smiles faintly.

He has felt this before.

The forest feels alive around him.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 02

The Dying Tree

Forest beyond the settlement — Afternoon

Aurel follows a narrow path deeper into the forest.

The trees become larger.

The sunlight becomes thinner.

Then he sees something that makes him stop.

The Ancient Tree

An enormous tree stands alone. Its bark is cracked. Its branches are dry. Almost every leaf has fallen.

Aurel slowly approaches.

He places his hand against the bark.

Aurel: "You're dying..."

He looks at the empty branches.

Aurel: "Why?"

Silence.

Aurel: "I don't know how to help you."

Aurel closes his eyes. He simply keeps his hand against the tree.

Then something changes.

The forest becomes completely silent.

A warmth travels through Aurel's hand.

His eyes open.

The Awakening

A faint emerald light spreads beneath Aurel's hand.

The light travels through the roots. The branches tremble. New leaves begin to form.

Aurel pulls his hand away.

The tree is alive again.

He stares at his own hand.

Aurel: "What... was that?"

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 03

The Shrine

Deep within the ancient forest

Aurel continues deeper into the forest.

He does not know why.

Something seems to be pulling him forward.

Through the trees, he discovers stone ruins.

An ancient shrine stands hidden beneath roots and moss.

The Ancient Symbol

At the centre of the shrine is the image of a great tree surrounded by four symbols.

Aurel touches the stone.

A strange feeling passes through him.

Beneath the tree symbol, something catches his attention.

He moves the loose stone aside.

Beneath it is a small dark stone marked with four mysterious symbols.

Aurel: "What is this?"

The moment his fingers touch the artifact, the four symbols briefly glow.

Aurel immediately pulls his hand away.

His breathing becomes faster.

He does not understand the artifact.

But something inside him tells him that it must remain hidden.

He places it back beneath the stone.

Then he hears a distant sound.

Footsteps.

Aurel: "Who's there?"

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 04

Something Has Awakened

The settlement — Evening

Aurel returns to the settlement before nightfall.

But something feels different.

The adults are whispering.

The animals are restless.

The guardian watches Aurel from across the settlement.

He notices something in the boy's eyes.

Father: "Where have you been?"

Aurel: "In the forest."

Father: "Alone?"

Aurel: "Yes."

The guardian studies him.

Father: "Did anything happen?"

Aurel hesitates.

Aurel: "I... found something."

Before Aurel can explain, a distant horn echoes through the mountains.

The guardian's expression changes.

He knows that sound.

Father: "Aurel..."

Aurel: "Father?"

Father: "Go inside."

Aurel: "Why?"

Father: "Now."

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 05

The Shadow Legion

The mountain settlement — Night

Darkness falls over the settlement.

Then the forest erupts with movement.

Figures emerge from between the trees.

Black armour. Dark banners. Weapons covered in strange markings.

The Shadow Legion has found them.

THE SHADOW LEGION

They are not here for the settlement. They are searching for something that has awakened.

Shadow Soldier: "Search every house."

Another Soldier: "The signal came from this mountain."

Shadow Soldier: "Find the source."

Aurel watches from inside the house.

His hands begin to tremble.

He remembers the tree.

He remembers the shrine.

The guardian enters.

Aurel: "Father... who are they?"

The guardian remains silent.

Aurel: "You know them."

Father: "I know what they are capable of."

Aurel: "Why did they come here?"

The guardian looks directly at him.

Father: "Because something has awakened."

Aurel's eyes widen.

He suddenly understands.

They came because of him.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 06

The Truth of the Guardian

The settlement — During the attack

Outside, the settlement begins to burn.

People run through the streets.

The Shadow Legion moves from house to house.

Aurel looks at the guardian.

Aurel: "You knew this would happen."

Father: "I feared it would."

Aurel: "Why?"

The guardian lowers his head.

Father: "Because I have been running from my past for many years."

Aurel: "What past?"

Father: "I once belonged to them."

Aurel freezes.

Outside, another building catches fire.

Aurel: "You were part of the Shadow Legion?"

Father: "Yes."

Aurel: "Then why did you leave?"

The guardian looks at him.

Father: "Because one day they ordered me to kill an innocent child."

Father: "I refused."

Father: "That was the day I became their enemy."

Aurel looks at him differently.

The guardian continues.

Father: "Years ago, I found you."

Aurel: "Found me?"

Father: "Inside an ancient shrine."

Aurel: "The shrine in the forest..."

The guardian nods.

Father: "I didn't know who you were."

Father: "I only knew that someone had left you there."

Father: "So I took you with me."

Aurel struggles to understand.

Before he can ask another question, the door shakes violently.

Someone is outside.
✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 07

The Escape

The burning settlement

The door breaks apart.

A Shadow Legion soldier enters.

The guardian moves instantly.

Steel clashes against steel.

Aurel steps backward.

Father: "Aurel!"

Aurel: "Father!"

Father: "Run!"

Aurel: "I'm not leaving you!"

Father: "You don't understand!"

Father: "They aren't looking for me."

He looks at Aurel.

Father: "They're looking for you."

Aurel's eyes widen.

The guardian grabs him and pushes him toward the rear exit.

Aurel: "Father!"

Father: "Go!"

Father: "And whatever happens..."

Father: "Don't let them take you."

Aurel runs.

He enters the forest.

But he does not go far.

He turns back.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 08

The Last Stand

Forest edge — overlooking the settlement

Aurel hides behind the trees.

From there, he can see the entire settlement.

Fire consumes the wooden houses.

Smoke rises into the night sky.

His Father stands alone.

The Guardian

The man who had protected Aurel for years now stands between the Shadow Legion and the boy he chose to protect.

Aurel grips the tree beside him.

His hand begins to glow.

The same emerald light from the ancient tree appears again.

The symbols beneath his skin seem to tremble.

Aurel: "What is happening to me?"

Across the settlement, the guardian looks toward the forest.

He sees Aurel.

For one brief moment, their eyes meet.

Father: "Aurel..."

Aurel's eyes fill with tears.

Aurel: "Father!"

The guardian turns back toward the soldiers.

He knows what he must do.

✦ ✦ ✦
SCENE 09

The End of One Life

The burning settlement — Night

The Shadow Legion surrounds the guardian.

He raises his weapon.

Even wounded, he refuses to step aside.

Aurel watches helplessly from the forest.

Shadow Soldier: "Where is the boy?"

The guardian says nothing.

Shadow Soldier: "Tell us where he went."

Father: "You'll never find him."

Another soldier approaches.

The guardian looks toward the forest one final time.

He knows Aurel is still watching.

Father: "Aurel..."

His voice carries faintly through the smoke.

Father: "You are my son."

Aurel freezes.

Tears run down his face.

The guardian smiles.

Then the attack comes.

FATHER

The guardian falls.

Aurel watches him die from the forest edge.

For several seconds, Aurel cannot move.

The world seems to disappear around him.

His home is burning.

His Father is dead.

And the people he knew are gone.

Aurel slowly clenches his fists.

Aurel: "Father..."

His voice breaks.

Aurel: "I'm sorry."

He wipes the tears from his face.

Aurel: "I'll come back for you."

Aurel turns away.

He runs.

Deeper into the ancient forest.

Behind him, the settlement burns.

Ahead of him lies a world he knows nothing about.

The forest closes around him.

And somewhere beneath the earth, something ancient begins to awaken.

END OF CHAPTER 1

Aurel has lost his home and the man who raised him. He now carries questions about his origin, the ancient shrine, the mysterious artifact, and the power awakening inside him.

But his journey has only just begun.
